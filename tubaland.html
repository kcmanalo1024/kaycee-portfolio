01 — OVERVIEW

From a manual laundry workflow to a digital experience.

Tubaland is a capstone laundry management application combining a customer-facing Android application with a web-based administrative interface to support laundry services and transactions.

My primary contribution was the UI/UX design and front-end development of the application. I worked on user flows, wireframes, interface layouts, visual design, and translating approved designs into functional Android screens.

01 / CUSTOMER

Book with less friction.

Service selection, add-ons, pickup and delivery options, scheduling, and payment are brought into one guided booking flow.

02 / OPERATIONS

Keep the workflow connected.

The admin side supports operational visibility while location-based functionality helps connect pickup and delivery activities.

Tubaland brings together the Android customer experience and web-based administration layer, supported by application services, a database, and location functionality.
06 — KEY FEATURES

Six moments that define the Tubaland experience.

Each feature is shown through one focused screen. The first five use a phone-sized presentation; the final feature uses the horizontal admin website layout.

01Mobile

Notifications

Customers receive updates regarding their laundry service.

02Mobile

Laundry Booking

03Mobile

Order Tracking

Customers can follow the status of their laundry order.

04Mobile

Rider / Delivery Tracking

Location-based functionality supports pickup and delivery tracking.

05Mobile

06Web Admin

07 — CHALLENGES & LEARNING

Designing for multiple roles taught me to think beyond the screen.

Challenge

One product, different responsibilities.

Tubaland required different experiences for customers, riders, and administrators. A key challenge was maintaining consistency between the prototype and the implemented Android interface while working within technical constraints.

Learning

Good interfaces connect systems.

The project strengthened my understanding of how UI/UX, development, databases, location-based functionality, testing, and collaboration connect to create a complete digital product.

08 — REFLECTION

Good design is not only about looking good. It has to work.

Tubaland allowed me to combine my interest in design with my technical background in Information Technology. From the first user flow to the functional application, I learned to consider both what users need and what developers can realistically build.
